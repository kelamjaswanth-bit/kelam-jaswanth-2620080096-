Legends of Indian Cricket

1. Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is one of the greatest batsmen in Indian cricket. He is famous for his batting skills, fitness and consistency. He is popularly known as King Kohli.

  • 2025 Champions Trophy Winner
  • 2025 IPL Winner

2. MS Dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni is one of the most successful captains in Indian cricket history. He is famous for his wicketkeeping, finishing skills and leadership. He is popularly known as Captain Cool.

  • Five-time IPL Champion as captain
  • One of India's most successful wicketkeepers

3. Rohit Sharma

Rohit Sharma is one of the finest opening batsmen in Indian cricket. He is famous for his powerful batting and record-breaking double centuries. He is popularly known as Hitman.

Conclusion

Virat Kohli, MS Dhoni and Rohit Sharma are inspirational players who have made Indian cricket proud through their talent and achievements.
